## What I am building

- **Data:** licence-checked Khmer read speech, cleaned conservatively, with forced alignment to cut voice prompts at natural pauses.
- **Text front-end:** a number reader for dates, times, money and percentages, with every rule checked against real recordings, and handling for mixed Khmer and Latin text.
- **Training:** fine-tuning Kyutai's [Pocket TTS](/blog/pocket-tts-calm-explained/) with a new Khmer tokenizer, each experiment planned in advance and compared on the same held-out speakers.
- **Evaluation:** automatic intelligibility and voice-similarity scores, plus blinded listening by a native speaker, because automatic judges miss pronunciation errors a listener hears. The [evaluation post](/blog/speech-evaluation-metrics/) explains why.

## Status

A working system exists. The data preparation and training are written up in [Same model, new language: fine-tuning Pocket TTS to speak Khmer](/blog/khmer-tts-fine-tuning/). Inference, the text front-end and pronunciation fixes will follow.
