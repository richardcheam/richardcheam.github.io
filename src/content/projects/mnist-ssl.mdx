## What I did

Two approaches, compared under the same splits, seeds and evaluation:

- **Semi-supervised:** train a CNN on the 100 labels, let it label the rest (pseudo-labelling), keep its confident guesses, and retrain, refreshing the labels as it improves.
- **Self-supervised:** pretrain with SimCLR, which learns features by making two augmented views of the same image agree, using no labels at all. Then train only a small classifier on top with the 100 labels.

## What I found

- **Self-supervised features won.** A plain linear classifier on SimCLR features reached 98.55%, above pseudo-labelling at 97.18%. A small MLP head on the same features gave 98.44%, so extra capacity on top did not help.
- **The remaining errors are mostly genuinely ambiguous digits**, found by inspecting low-confidence predictions.
